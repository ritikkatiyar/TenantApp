# Inventory Move-In/Move-Out Checklist Plan

## Overview
Release 1 focuses on the core inventory lifecycle: assignment, verification, damage assessment, and deposit settlement integration.

## Screens to Generate
1. **Property Inventory List (Owner)**: Comprehensive view of all items, filtered by status (Assigned, Shared, Due for Service).
2. **Lease Move-In Assignment (Owner)**: Checklist for documenting item condition at the start of a lease.
3. **Move-Out Verification & Settlement (Owner)**: Comparative view of move-in vs move-out condition with damage deduction calculations and final deposit refund preview.
4. **My Unit Inventory (Tenant)**: Read-only view for tenants to see what items are assigned to their unit and property-wide amenities.

## Key Logic
- Event-driven move-out checklist creation.
- Owner approval workflow for deductions.
- Automatic ledger entry creation for adjustments and refunds.
